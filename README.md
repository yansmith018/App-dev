# My favorite series: Falling into your smile

> "In the world of E-Sport, love and competition collide"

## Overview
**Falling into your smile** is a Chinese romantic comedy and e-sport drama released in 2021
The story takes place in the exciting and competitive world of **professional gaming**, where passion, friendship, and love intertwine. The series quickly gained international popularity for its refreshing theme, charming cast, and positive representation of women in e-sports. 
The series beautifully combines the excitement of competitive gaming with the sweetness of young love and personal growth.

## Main Characters
- **Cheng Xiao(Tong Yao)** - first female professional gamer in the ZGDX team.
- **Xu Kai(Lu Sicheng)** - cold yet talented captain of ZGDX.
- **Zhai Xiaowen(Lu Yue)** - witty and charming top-laner.
- **Sun Kai(Chen Jin Yang)** - Tong Yao’s supportive best friend.

## Plot Summary
When **Tong Yao** joins the all-male e-sports team **ZGDX**, she faces challenges to prove that girls can also compete at the highest level.  
Along the way, she finds **friendship, teamwork**, and a **slow-burn romance** with Lu Sicheng.
The story highlights **the challenges of teamwork, personal growth, and love** in a world dominated by competition.  

## Fun Facts
- The cast trained with real e-sports players to make the gameplay scenes look authentic.  
- Many scenes were inspired by real e-sports tournaments.  
- The drama gained millions of views in its first week of release and became a hit across Asia.

## Why I love it
It promote the empowerment of the women and the relationship between the male lead and female lead is sweet and funny. The style of the show have the aesthetic, lively editing, and make it enjoyable even for gamers and non-gamers.
